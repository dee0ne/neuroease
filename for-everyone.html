For everyone

What neurotypical people get from ND-friendly design.

This is the curb-cut effect: features built for accessibility end up helping everyone.

30%

Neurodiverse teams can be up to 30% more productive when given the right environment and tools.

63%

Of employees rank flexible work among the top 4 benefits. Originally an ND accommodation, now a universal preference.

36%

Companies with inclusive neurodiversity programmes report 36% higher retention across all employees.

Sources: Galt Staffing, SHRM 2024, McKinsey Diversity Matters 2023.

The same principles that make a tool work for a neurodivergent brain — clear structure, low clutter, predictable navigation, adjustable pace — measurably improve outcomes for everyone. This is not a trade-off. Designing for cognitive diversity raises the baseline for all users, the same way curb cuts (designed for wheelchair users) benefit parents with strollers, delivery workers, and rolling luggage. When you build for the edges, the centre works better too.
