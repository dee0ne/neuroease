Get to know us

Built in the open, for everyone.

NeuroEase is a productivity tool designed by and for neurodivergent people. We started because every to-do list, planner, and focus app assumed a brain that could plan, initiate, and sustain attention on command. That assumption is the problem — we're here to fix it.

Community-driven

Every feature starts with a real need from the ND community. We build with input, not assumptions.

Open source

The code is public. Contributions, audits, and forks are welcome — transparency builds trust.

Neurodivergent-first

We design for ADHD, autism, dyslexia, and related cognitive variations first. Everyone else benefits too.
