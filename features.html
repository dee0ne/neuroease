What makes NeuroEase different

Not just another mood tracker.

Most wellbeing apps assume you can notice, name, and report an emotion. For many neurodivergent people, that assumption is the barrier. Here is what we do instead.

Daily Check-in

Asks what your body is doing — tight chest, racing thoughts, heavy limbs — then suggests the feeling for you, instead of demanding you name it yourself.

Built for alexithymia, not against it.

Energy Map

Tracks your capacity across social, sensory, cognitive, and physical domains separately — not one flat mood score.

Makes adaptive scheduling actually adaptive.

Task Breaker

Breaks any task into micro-steps, plus a shrink button for the smallest honest version when even that feels like too much.

Removes the activation energy barrier to starting.

Focus Guard

Detects when you are in hyperfocus and protects it — fewer interruptions, gentler wind-down warnings, no arbitrary timer cutting you off.

Built for monotropism, not against it.

Spiral Check

A fast tool for moments of rejection-sensitive dysphoria: separates "what happened" from "what my brain is telling me it means."

No other mood app addresses RSD by name.

Patterns

Finds correlations automatically — "your energy drops on days logged as loud or bright" — surfaced in plain language, no journaling required.

Pattern recognition without forcing writing.

State Card

One tap generates a summary you can share: "today I have low capacity for talking, high capacity for focused work." No diagnosis disclosure needed.

A communication tool, not just a private log.

Masking Tracker

Optionally log when you are masking and how draining it was. Over time, see which environments cost the most — visually and clearly.

Turns an invisible burden into something visible and plannable.
