A productivity tool that adapts to your brain

We built this because to-do lists were breaking our brains.

Standard productivity tools assume a brain that can plan, start, and focus on command. For neurodivergent people, that assumption is the problem. NeuroEase is a different approach, backed by research and built with the community.

Key features

Energy Map

Tracks capacity across social, sensory, cognitive, and physical domains separately — not one flat score.

Task Breaker

Breaks any task into micro-steps with a shrink button for the smallest honest version.

Spiral Check

For moments of rejection-sensitive dysphoria: separates fact from what the brain says it means.

Masking Tracker

Log when you mask and how draining it was. See which environments cost the most.

Strategies that work today

1

Break tasks into tiny steps

Start with "put one dish in the sink." Absurdly small steps remove the barrier to starting.

Task Breaker
2

Make time visible

Use a visual timer so you don't have to track it internally.

Focus Guard
3

Schedule by energy, not clock time

Assign tasks to high and low energy states instead of fighting natural rhythms.

Energy Map
4

Lower your sensory input

Noise-cancelling headphones, dimmed lights, and decluttering reduce cognitive load.
