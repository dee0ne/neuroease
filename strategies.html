Strategies that work

General techniques you can use today.

These are not app features. They are research-backed strategies anyone can apply right now, with or without NeuroEase.

Not every strategy works for every brain. Try what fits. Skip what does not.
1

Break tasks into tiny steps

Instead of "clean the kitchen," start with "put one dish in the sink." Absurdly small steps remove the activation barrier to starting.

NeuroEase helps with: Task Breaker
2

Make time visible

Time blindness makes abstract time unreliable. Use a visual timer or progress bar so you do not have to track it internally.

NeuroEase helps with: Focus Guard
3

Use body doubling

Having someone present — physically or virtually — measurably improves focus and follow-through by reducing the emotional weight of starting.

NeuroEase helps with: Daily Check-in
4

Schedule by energy, not clock time

Assign tasks to energy states: high-focus work when you focus best, low-demand tasks during slumps. Do not fight your natural rhythms.

NeuroEase helps with: Energy Map
5

Lower your sensory input

Noise-cancelling headphones, dimmed lights, visual decluttering, and quiet spaces reduce cognitive load for everyone.

NeuroEase helps with: State Card
6

Write it down, put it where you see it

Working memory runs out fast. Externalising tasks, instructions, and deadlines onto something visible frees mental resources for actual work.

NeuroEase helps with: Task Breaker
