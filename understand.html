Understanding neurodivergence

Neurodivergence is variation, not deficit.

An estimated 15-20% of people process information, attention, and emotion differently. Here is what that means in plain terms.

  • Neurodivergence describes natural variations in how the brain processes information, manages attention, regulates emotion, and interacts with the world.
  • Includes ADHD, autism, dyslexia, dyspraxia, Tourette syndrome, and related cognitive differences.
  • The difficulty comes from mismatch between a person and their environment, not from something broken inside the person.
  • Executive functions are the brain's management system: planning, task initiation, time management, working memory, emotional regulation.
  • Executive dysfunction means knowing you need to start a task but being unable to make your body begin. It is not laziness — it is a neurological barrier.
  • Time blindness, emotional flooding after rejection (RSD), and overwhelm from multi-step instructions are common experiences.
  • Many autistic people have heightened or reduced sensitivity to light, sound, touch, smell, or internal body signals (interoception).
  • Alexithymia — difficulty identifying or naming emotions — makes standard mood trackers that ask "how do you feel?" inaccessible.
  • Monotropism: intense, sustained focus on one interest. Rigid timers interrupt this instead of supporting it.
  • NeuroEase takes the neurodiversity stance: neurological variation is a normal and valuable part of human diversity.
  • The goal is not to make neurodivergent people function like neurotypical people. The goal is to build tools and environments that accommodate different cognitive styles.
  • Companies that embrace neurodiversity consistently outperform their peers on innovation, retention, and productivity.
About symbols. The rainbow infinity loop was created by the autistic community in 2005 and represents neurodivergence broadly. Gold (Au, the chemical symbol for gold) represents autism acceptance. Orange represents ADHD awareness. Green represents mental health awareness. This project uses none as default UI colors — identity markers should be chosen by the user, never applied by the product.
